# Privacy Policy - NotyAlert

**Last updated**: October 20, 2026  
**Application**: NotyAlert (Android)

---

At **NotyAlert**, we respect and protect your privacy. NotyAlert was designed and built under the core principle of **Privacy by Design** and 100% offline-first functionality.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled within the **NotyAlert** mobile application for Android.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
- **No external servers**: NotyAlert does **not** collect, upload, share, or sell any personal data, identifiers, or telemetry.
- **No accounts or sign-up**: You can use all features without creating an account or providing an email address.
- **Your reminders remain yours**: Titles, notes, alert times, and settings never leave your physical device.

### 2. Local Storage & User Ownership
- All data generated by the user is stored exclusively in the local SQLite/Room database within your device's private sandbox.
- Backup, JSON export/import, and QR code transfers occur 100% locally on your phone without external cloud intervention.

### 3. Declared Permissions & Justification
NotyAlert requests only the permissions strictly required to perform its core alarm and reminder functionalities:

| Permission | Technical Justification |
| :--- | :--- |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Delivers sound and visual alerts at the exact scheduled time (Android 13+). |
| `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` & `USE_EXACT_ALARM` | Required by `AlarmManager` to ensure alerts trigger with alarm-clock precision even when the device is locked. |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Allows the system receiver to automatically restore and reschedule active alarms if your device reboots. |
| `CAMERA` *(Optional)* | Used exclusively to scan backup or reminder transfer QR codes. Operates in real time; **no images or videos are recorded, stored, or transmitted**. |
| `VIBRATE` | Provides subtle haptic feedback for buttons and calendar interactions. |
| `REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS` | Requests exemption from aggressive background process killing by manufacturer layers (MIUI, One UI, EMUI). |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Briefly wakes the CPU at the exact millisecond an alarm triggers to deliver notifications reliably. |

### 4. Zero Third-Party Trackers or Advertisements
- NotyAlert does **not** include analytics libraries (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Google Analytics).
- NotyAlert contains **no** advertising networks (AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.).
- NotyAlert contains **no** social media SDKs or behavioral trackers.

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights
Because we keep no copies of your data on servers:
- You can delete individual reminders inside the app at any time.
- You can permanently erase all data at any time via Android Settings: `Settings > Apps > NotyAlert > Storage > Clear Data`, or by simply uninstalling the application.

### 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)
NotyAlert is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). Operating entirely offline without external storage guarantees a secure digital environment for families and minors.

### 7. Changes to this Policy
We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or legal changes. Updates will be communicated via version release notes on Google Play Store and inside the application's legal section.

### 8. Support & Inquiries
For questions, suggestions, or support regarding NotyAlert, official support channels are available directly through the app listing on the Google Play Store.
